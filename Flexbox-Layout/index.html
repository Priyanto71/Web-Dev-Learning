Naruto

Naruto Uzumaki is the energetic and optimistic protagonist who, as an orphan ostracized for hosting the Nine-Tailed Fox (Kurama) sealed within him, strives to become Hokage, the leader of the Hidden Leaf Village. Initially a mischievous troublemaker seeking attention, his boisterous and resilient personality allows him to form deep bonds, transforming his desire for recognition into a genuine commitment to protect his friends and earn the village's respect.
